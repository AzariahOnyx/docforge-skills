# Pause your in-app alerts for 1 hour

Use Quiet Hours when you want to pause your own in-app alerts for one hour. <!-- evidence: C02, C03 -->

## Before you begin

- Sign in to Pulseboard. <!-- evidence: C07 -->
- Connect to the internet; pausing is available only while online. <!-- evidence: C06 -->

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**. <!-- evidence: C03 -->
2. Select **Pause alerts**. <!-- evidence: C03 -->
3. Choose **1 hour**. <!-- evidence: C03 -->
4. Select **Confirm**. <!-- evidence: C03 -->

## Expected result

The screen displays the selected end time and **Resume now**. Your in-app alerts are paused for the selected period; email alerts are unaffected. In-app alerts resume automatically when the period ends. To restart them sooner, select **Resume now**. <!-- evidence: C02, C04, C05 -->
