# Quiet Hours

Quiet Hours lets you pause your own in-app alerts in Pulseboard for a selected period. Pulseboard is a team dashboard where members receive in-app alerts for items assigned to them.

## How it works

Open **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**, select **Pause alerts**, choose **2 hours** or **Until tomorrow**, then select **Confirm**. The screen displays the selected end time and **Resume now**. Alerts resume automatically when the selected period ends. Selecting **Resume now** restarts in-app alerts immediately and requires an online connection.

## Before you begin

You must be signed in as a member and online to pause alerts. Any signed-in member can change their own Quiet Hours setting.

## Important behavior

- Quiet Hours does not affect email alerts or another member's settings.
- An admin cannot change another member's setting.
- **Resume now** requires an online connection. The PRD separately specifies that pausing is available only while online; it does not state an online requirement for automatic resumption.
