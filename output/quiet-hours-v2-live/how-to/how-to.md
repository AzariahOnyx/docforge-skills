# Pause your in-app alerts for two hours

Use Quiet Hours to pause your own Pulseboard in-app alerts for two hours.

## Before you begin

You must be signed in as a member and online to pause alerts.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**.
2. Select **Pause alerts**.
3. Choose **2 hours**.
4. Select **Confirm**.

## Expected result

The screen displays the selected end time and **Resume now**. Your in-app alerts resume automatically when the selected period ends. To restart them sooner, select **Resume now** while online.

Quiet Hours does not affect email alerts or another member's settings.
