# Pause in-app alerts with Quiet Hours

Quiet Hours lets a Pulseboard member pause their own in-app alerts for a selected period. It does not pause email alerts or change another member's settings.

## How it works

From **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**, select **Pause alerts** and choose **1 hour** or **Until tomorrow**. After you confirm, the screen displays the selected end time and a **Resume now** action. In-app alerts resume automatically when the selected period ends, or immediately if you select **Resume now**.

When you choose **Until tomorrow**, check the end time shown on the screen after confirming.

## Access and availability

Any signed-in member can change their own Quiet Hours setting. An admin cannot change another member's setting. Pausing alerts requires an online connection.

To pause your own alerts for a supported fixed period, see [Pause in-app alerts for 1 hour](../how-to/how-to.md).
