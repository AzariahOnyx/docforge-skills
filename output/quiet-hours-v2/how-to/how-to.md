# Pause your in-app alerts for 2 hours

Use Quiet Hours when you want to pause your own in-app alerts for two hours.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to Pulseboard.
- Connect to the internet; pausing is available only while online.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**.
2. Select **Pause alerts**.
3. Choose **2 hours**.
4. Select **Confirm**.

## Expected result

The screen displays the selected end time and **Resume now**. Your in-app alerts are paused for the selected period; email alerts are unaffected. In-app alerts resume automatically when the period ends. To restart them sooner while online, select **Resume now**.
