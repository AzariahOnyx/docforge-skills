# Quiet Hours: pause in-app alerts

Members can now pause their own Pulseboard in-app alerts for **2 hours** or **Until tomorrow** from notification settings. Email alerts and other members' settings are unchanged.

- The screen shows the selected end time and a **Resume now** action.
- In-app alerts resume when the period ends or when the member selects **Resume now**.
- Pausing requires an online connection.
- **Resume now** also requires an online connection.
